Kisanloop is an AI app that helps farmers turn leftover crop residue into income by matching it with nearby buyers, and uses low-cost air sensors to show the air getting cleaner.

See how it works

Farmers burn residue because selling it is hard

After harvest, farmers have a short window to clear their fields. Burning is the fastest and cheapest option, and it fills the air with PM2.5 across north India, including Bihar.

There are buyers for this straw: biomass plants, bio-CNG units, cattle-feed makers and paper mills. Farmers just can't find them, price the residue or arrange pickup in time.

No buyer accessFarmers don't know who is buying nearby.
No fair priceRates change by place and week.
No proofNobody measures local air before and after.

How Kisanloop works

  1. Farmer sends a photo or voice noteIn Hindi, through the app or WhatsApp. No typing needed.
  2. AI estimates the residueType of crop and quantity, read from the photo.
  3. We match a nearby buyerWith a fair price and a pickup date.
  4. Sensors check the airLow-cost PM2.5 sensors show if burning went down in the village.

Built with Microsoft Azure AI
